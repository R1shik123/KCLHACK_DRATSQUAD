    How this works

    • Roads: OpenStreetMap main roads in Greater London (motorway to unclassified), with posted speed limits or class defaults.
    • Trips: a fixed, seeded sample of car trips between main-road junctions. Closing a road reroutes only the trips that used it, to the fastest open route.
    • Time: free-flow time plus a wait at each signalised junction, multiplied by a daytime congestion factor.
    • Cost: given as a low / central / high range, because the number of real trips each sample trip stands for, people per car and value of time are all uncertain.
    • Not modelled: queues building up on detour roads, buses, pedestrians, people cancelling trips.
