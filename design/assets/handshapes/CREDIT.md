# Handshape pictures

The 26 ASL fingerspelling drawings `A.svg` … `Z.svg` are **public domain**, from the wpclipart.com set, downloaded from
Wikimedia Commons (`File:Sign_language_A.svg` … `File:Sign_language_Z.svg`, licence shown there as "Public domain").
They were checked for scripts and external links before being added. J and Z show the motion as an arrow.

Used by both platforms: the website loads them from `design/assets/handshapes/`; the app bundles them.
Where they appear: hint cards, the faint "ghost" overlay on the camera during a hint (always on in Practice and on the
letter page), the Learn grid and the letter page.
